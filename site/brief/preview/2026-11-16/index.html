Weekly energy brief · 16 November 2026

Energy prices this week: 30 plans up, 9 down

In the seven days to 16 November 2026, retailers changed prices on 89 plans: 30 home plans now cost a typical household more, 9 cost less and 50 home plans changed in ways that don't affect a typical bill, such as fees or feed-in tariffs. Retailers launched 425 new plans and withdrew 556 plans. The biggest rise was on AGL's Residential Seniors (gas): up to $162 a year for a typical home. The biggest cut was on Momentum Energy's Standing Offer (electricity): up to $86 a year less.

How this is worked out

A typical home uses 4,000 kWh of electricity or 20,000 MJ of gas a year, spread over the day in a typical pattern. Yearly costs include GST and discounts everyone gets, but not solar, controlled load or conditional discounts. Changes compare each plan's prices on 9 November 2026 with 16 November 2026, using the same calculator as the plan finder. Prices are as published by each retailer through the Consumer Data Right. Business plans and demand tariffs are counted but not priced. Methodology.
