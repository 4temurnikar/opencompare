Energy prices this week: 4 plans up, 6 down

In the seven days to 23 November 2026, retailers changed prices on 269 plans: 4 home plans now cost a typical household more, 6 cost less, 101 home plans changed in ways that don't affect a typical bill, such as fees or feed-in tariffs, 36 home plans changed that we can't price for a typical home, such as demand tariffs and 122 business plans changed. Retailers launched 193 new plans and withdrew 194 plans. The biggest rise was on Sumo's Sumo Standing Offer (electricity): up to $1 a year for a typical home. The biggest cut was on Sumo's Sumo Standing Offer (electricity): up to $276 a year less.

How this is worked out

A typical home uses 4,000 kWh of electricity or 20,000 MJ of gas a year, spread over the day in a typical pattern. Yearly costs include GST and discounts everyone gets, but not solar, controlled load or conditional discounts. Changes compare each plan's prices on 16 November 2026 with 23 November 2026, using the same calculator as the plan finder. Prices are as published by each retailer through the Consumer Data Right. Business plans and demand tariffs are counted but not priced. Methodology.
