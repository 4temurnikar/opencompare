Privacy

OpenCompare is run by the operator of OpenCompare. This page explains what information the site collects and what happens to it.

Using the site

You don't need an account. Postcodes, usage figures and other details you enter in the plan finders are used in your own browser to work out results; they aren't sent to us or stored by us. We don't use advertising or analytics trackers, and we don't set cookies.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, receives your IP address and browser details when you load a page, under GitHub's privacy statement. The site's fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives the same technical details under Google's privacy policy.

The weekly email

An email version of the weekly brief is being set up. Until then, no email addresses are collected.

Questions, corrections and complaints

Contact the contact address on this page (being set up). If you're not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.
